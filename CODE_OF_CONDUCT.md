# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in repo-vital to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good faith; ask a question before assuming a slight.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may say no.
- Give credit for ideas that are not yours.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, or dismissive comments about someone's skill.
- Harassment in public or private channels, including unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you
represent the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainer at the address on my GitHub profile. Reports
are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or delete comments, and may block
accounts that repeatedly violate this code.
